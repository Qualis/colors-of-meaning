# The Lantern-Keeper's Daughter

## Chapter 1 — The Quiet Harbour

Mara has kept her father's lighthouse since the fever took him, trimming the wick each dusk and
polishing the great brass lens until it throws its beam clean across the black water. The village
below trusts the light and little else about her. She is content with the gulls, the salt wind, and
the slow turning of the seasons, and she tells herself she wants nothing more than this steady,
solitary work above the waves.

## Chapter 2 — The Wreck on the Rocks

A winter gale drives a merchant ship onto the reef, and Mara rows out through the breaking surf to
haul a half-drowned stranger from the foam. He carries a sealed letter and a fever of his own, and
in his delirium he speaks of a city she has only heard of in stories. For the first time the narrow
world of the lighthouse feels too small, and a restless, frightening curiosity begins to stir in
her chest as she watches over him through the long dark night.

## Chapter 3 — The Letter's Secret

When the stranger wakes he begs Mara to carry his letter inland, for men are hunting him and the
message must reach the capital before the spring assizes. She refuses, then relents, then lies awake
weighing duty against fear. Leaving the light unattended is unthinkable, yet the wick of her old
life has already begun to gutter. At dawn she banks the lamp, packs bread and her father's knife,
and turns her back on the only home she has ever known.

## Chapter 4 — The Long Road

The road inland is nothing like the sea. Mara crosses frost-cracked moors and crowded market towns,
learns to barter and to hide, and twice narrowly escapes the riders who trail the stranger's scent.
Hunger and cold wear at her, but each night she practises the stranger's words until she could
recite the letter from memory, and slowly the terrified girl from the harbour hardens into someone
who can meet a stranger's eye and lie without flinching.

## Chapter 5 — Quarterly Earnings Review

The regional distribution division reported a four percent decline in gross margin for the fiscal
quarter, driven primarily by elevated freight costs and an unfavourable product mix. Management
reaffirmed full-year guidance and announced a share repurchase programme, while analysts questioned
the sustainability of the dividend given softening demand in the industrial segment. The board will
revisit capital allocation priorities at the next scheduled meeting of the audit committee.

## Chapter 6 — The Capital in Flames

Mara reaches the capital to find its gates barred and its markets seething with rumour. She threads
the riots by night, betrayed once and sheltered twice by strangers, until she stands at last before
the magistrate with the letter in her shaking hand. The words she memorised on the road spill out of
her, and the room goes silent. Everything she abandoned the lighthouse for now balances on whether a
powerful man chooses to believe a girl who smells of the sea.

## Chapter 7 — A New Light

The letter is heard, the hunted stranger cleared, and the machinery of power grinds on without
thanking her. Mara could stay in the bright, dangerous city, but she finds she is homesick for the
turning beam and the honest dark. She returns to the harbour changed, relights the lamp with her own
hands, and understands at last that keeping a light is not hiding from the world but calling the lost
of it safely home.
